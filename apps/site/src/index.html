Personal League of Legends performance

See your own games clearly.

LanePulse pulls your match history from the Riot Games API, scores each game against targets for your role, lets you set one goal before you queue, shows your own live stats on your phone while you play, and sums up your last hundred games. Only your data. Never a tip.

Matches

Your recent games with a score on a 1–10 ladder, the result, your champion and lane opponent. Tap one for a review of farming, combat, vision and lane, each with the target it was measured against.

Prepare

Before a game, pick a champion and a role and set a single goal, like 7.0 CS per minute. LanePulse remembers it and tells you afterwards whether you met it.

Live

The desktop companion reads the game you are playing from Riot’s local Live Client Data API and relays your own stats to your phone over a private channel. You see your numbers and how they compare to your goal. Nothing about the enemy team that the game does not already show you.

Performance

Your last 100 games in one place: average score, recent form against your own average, strongest and weakest role, the area that holds you back, your champions and the lane opponents you lose to most.

What LanePulse does not do

  • No in-game instructions or tactical advice, in the app or in notifications.
  • No data about other players, no leaderboards, no comparisons with other accounts.
  • No information about the current game that the game itself does not show you.
  • No sale of data, no ads, no paid tier.

How it uses the Riot Games API

Match data is fetched once per game and cached. Your match list refreshes while you look at it, and your last hundred games are downloaded once per account. The live view uses the Live Client Data API on your own computer, not a Riot server.
